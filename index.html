About the Panchayat

Know Nilamel

Understanding the landscape, people and development priorities of Nilamel Grama Panchayat.

A Community-Oriented Local Government

Nilamel Grama Panchayat is situated in the Chadayamangalam Block of Kollam district in Kerala. The region forms part of Kerala's midland landscape, characterised by a combination of hills, valleys, agricultural land, streams and settlements.

The panchayat plays an important role in local administration, rural development, public welfare and the delivery of essential civic services. Its responsibilities include supporting community infrastructure, sanitation, public health, drinking-water facilities and locally planned development programmes.

People's participation is an important element of decentralised governance. Through local planning and community involvement, the panchayat can address local needs while promoting environmental protection, social inclusion and sustainable resource management.

Our Direction

Vision and Mission

Working towards a well-served, inclusive and environmentally responsible community.

Our Vision

To support the development of a model local community that values equality, public participation, social justice and environmental sustainability. The vision emphasises improved quality of life, responsible use of local resources and opportunities for all sections of society.

Our Mission

To strengthen local development through responsive public services, community participation and effective planning. Priorities include essential infrastructure, sanitation, healthcare, education, livelihood opportunities, welfare initiatives and environmentally responsible development.
